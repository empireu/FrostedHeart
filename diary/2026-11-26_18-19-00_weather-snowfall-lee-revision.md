## Completed

**Why.** After the first in-game check of piece 3, two flaws showed in a blizzard: (1) straight-path scanning of raw heights shaded large plains behind rounded hills (roughly five times the hill's height downwind at `c ~ 0.21`), whereas air flows smoothly over broad landforms and separates only at sharp relief; and (2) deep ravines open to the sky stayed as foggy as exposed terrain, even though deep narrow cavities hold less wind-driven snow haze. In addition, the flat-ground fade shortcut (`h <= H`) leaked full snow beneath overhanging lips in cross-wind lee, and the fog culling bound did not account for near-field shelter or thinner cavity fog.

**What changed.**
- **Smoothed macro terrain surface `S` and relief lee scan (`SnowLee`, `SnowLeeMap`, `snow_smooth_build.fsh`, `snow_lee_build.fsh`, `FHShaders`).**
  - `SnowLeeMap` builds a 512 by 512 macro terrain heightmap `S` (1 texel = 2 by 2 blocks covering the 1024 by 1024 `FarHeights` window) in 3 passes at 1 pass per frame via `snow_smooth_build.fsh`: a valid-only 2x2 downsample emitting `(sum, count)` into `GL_RG32F`, followed by horizontal and vertical normalized Gaussians (`sigma = 8` coarse texels = 16 blocks, radius 24 coarse texels = 48 blocks) that divide by accumulated valid weight so `FarHeights.UNKNOWN` borders never pull `S` down. `S` rebuilds only when the `FarHeights` window moves or `noLeavesChangeCount` changes, never on wind turns, and swaps atomically only when no lee scan is reading it.
  - `snow_lee_build.fsh` now runs a 9-pass dual-channel `GL_RG32F` scan (`REACH = 256`, strides `1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128`) tracking both raw height `h_raw = max(H - c * t)` and relief `e_rel = max((H - S) - c * t)`. The final pass takes the 3-texel across-wind minimum of both channels, writes `R = min(h_raw, S + e_rel)` so rounded hills drop their lee while walls, houses, and cliff rims keep theirs (and the upwind foot of a cliff gets no false lee), and writes `G = clamp(w . grad S, -1, 1)` from central differences on `S` (`+-2` blocks).
  - `SnowLeeMap` tracks separate `SkyDrawTimer` timers for smooth passes and lee passes and reports both in `/calculatedSky snow`.
- **Terrain-following flakes, 6-face flux-weighted box re-entry, and overhang fix (`SnowLee`, `snow_lee.glsl`, `snow_flake_update.fsh`, `snow_flakes_draw.glsl`, `SnowfallRenderer`).**
  - Active (non-stopped) flakes gain vertical velocity `(w . grad S) * windSpeed * heightFade` with a linear height fade `1 - clamp((y - S) / 32, 0, 1)`, keeping peak vertical divergence minimal while letting flakes rise over upwind slopes and follow downwind slopes instead of colliding into hills and carving false shadows. Over lee columns (`h > groundFloor`), lee sinking lowers vertical velocity to at most `-(leeFallSpeed + max(0, -(w . grad S) * windSpeed))`, blending back over `h - groundFloor` above `h`. Both `snow_flake_update.fsh` and `snow_flakes_draw.glsl` use the same `snowLeeVerticalVelocityAt` helper and bind the published `S` texture (`SMOOTH_HEIGHT_UNIT = 8`, `SnowfallRenderer` now saves/restores 9 texture units) sampled with valid-only 4-tap bilinear filtering so `NO_HEIGHT` texels never bleed.
  - When `hasLeeMap != 0` and a flake leaves the camera's periodic box, `snowReenterBoxOffset` (Java twin `SnowLee.reenterBoxOffset`) places it on a flux-weighted inflow face using the horizontal relative step and the actual top-face and bottom-face vertical velocities at the camera column (with `frameCounter` hashing). This prevents vertical velocity gradients on hills from compounding across box crossings, starving the lower-downwind corner on upwind slopes, or piling flakes at the top face on downwind slopes.
  - Overhang fix: `snowLeeShare` / `SnowLee.leeShare` applies the flat-ground shortcut (`h <= H` gives full snow) only when `worldY >= columnHeight - 1.0`, preserving snow layers, slabs, and stairs on open ground. Below an overhang (`worldY < columnHeight - 1.0`), a flake below `h` gets share 0.0, while above `h` it fades against `groundFloor = min(columnHeight, S)` so bridges over open plains keep snowfall underneath.
- **Ravine cavity enclosure map and fog thinning (`FogCavity`, `FogCavityMap`, `fog_cavity_build.fsh`, `SkySetting`, `snow_walk.glsl`, `SnowFogWalk`, `WeatherFogRenderer`).**
  - Falling snow, flake count, flake shelter (`shareVolume`), and splats are completely unchanged inside open-sky ravines.
  - `FogCavityMap` and `fog_cavity_build.fsh` compute a wind-direction-free 512 by 512 opposing-wall enclosure surface `h_cav` around the `FarHeights` center in 12 passes at 1 pass per frame (cardinal reach 32 blocks with strides `1, 2, 4, 8, 16`; diagonal reach 23 diagonal steps = 32.53 blocks with strides `1, 2, 4, 8, 7`; constant enclosure slope `0.65`, with 3-texel perpendicular minimum on each axis so 1-wide and 2-wide posts do not create cavities). Along each of the 4 axes (`+-X`, `+-Z`, `+-(X+Z)`, `+-(X-Z)`), the axis height is `min(h_pos, h_neg)`, and `h_cav` is the maximum across the 4 axes. Rebuilds run only when the `FarHeights` window moves or `noLeavesChangeCount` changes, never when wind turns or changes speed.
  - Added setting `fogCavityShare` to `SkySetting` (default `0.5`, range `0.0` to `1.0`). Below `h_cav`, fog density multiplies by `effectiveFloor + (1 - effectiveFloor) * exp(-(h_cav - y) / decayLength)`, where `effectiveFloor = 1 - smoothstep(0.5, 6.0, windSpeed) * (1 - fogCavityShare)` fades thinning out smoothly in calm wind. This factor is applied only inside `snowWalkNearCells` and `snowWalkFarCells` (`snow_walk.glsl` and `SnowFogWalk`), leaving `snowWalkCellShareDirect` and `shareVolume` untouched. `WeatherFogRenderer` steps `FogCavityMap` outside the fog frame timer, binds the cavity texture on unit 8 (`SkyGlPass(9)`), reads it back during `/calculatedSky fog selftest` so CPU and GPU agree, and reports cavity build/pass timing in `/calculatedSky fog`.
- **Culling bound safety (`FogCullingDistance`, `WeatherFogRenderer`).**
  - Added an 11-argument `FogCullingDistance.opaqueDistance` overload taking `clearAirDensity`, `clearRange`, `cavityDensity`, `cavityRange`, and `shelterRange` (`regionHalfWidth`), integrating an exact piecewise-constant lower-bound extinction profile over sorted breakpoints `[0, shelterRange, clearRange, cavityRange, distance]` so the fog culling distance never clips terrain revealed by near-field shelter (branch review finding 8) or ravine cavity thinning. `WeatherFogRenderer` passes `regionHalfWidth`, `effectiveFloor` (evaluated with `max(wind.speed(), conditions.windSpeed())` during `RenderFog`), and `fogFroxelRange` into the culling distance and reports them in `describeBound()`.
- **Tests (`SnowLeeGameTests`, `FogCavityGameTests`, `FogSettingsGameTests`).**
  - `SnowLeeGameTests` now covers 5 tests: smoothed terrain + relief lee scan against brute force across partial `UNKNOWN` borders, plain, rounded 60-block hill leaving the plain its snow, 45-degree ridge, 20-block cliff (both downwind lee and zero upwind-foot lee), 8x8 house, 7-wide wall, 45-degree diagonal wall, 1-block tower at 0/30/45 degrees, 2-wide pillar, and 3-wide wall; 1024 by 1024 reach-256 non-wrap scan; overhang share, terrain-following velocity + lee sinking, and smooth/lee rebuild triggers; 16384-flake 300-frame sub-box density simulation over a hill (plain, upwind slope, crest, downwind slope); and GLSL constant parity across all 8 snow and cavity shader files.
  - `FogCavityGameTests` adds 2 tests: 4-axis cavity scan against brute force (ravines at 0, 22.5, and 45 degrees, single cliff foot, 80-wide valley, 1:2 V-valley, 1-wide and 2-wide post pairs, calm wind, and `fogCavityShare` 0/0.5/1); and `SnowFogWalk` cavity thinning (near + far walks, untouched `cellShare`/`heightShare`, above-rim open air, zero warm allocation) plus `FogCullingDistance` safety in uniform and height-varying blizzards.
  - `FogSettingsGameTests` checks `FOG_CAVITY_SHARE`.
