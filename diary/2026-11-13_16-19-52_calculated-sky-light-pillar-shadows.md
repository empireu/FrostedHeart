## Completed

Light pillars now find their lights and their shadows from world changes instead of polling, and blocks around a light shadow its pillar. Three steps on `feature/calculated-sky`, after the previous entry, `2026-11-13_16-19-01_calculated-sky-diamond-dust-light-pillars.md`.

- `8a548e97e`: `LightPillarWorldChanges` receives every client-side world change the pillars depend on, through `ClientLevelMixin_LightPillars` (`ClientLevel.setBlocksDirty`, which also sees pistons, fills, and explosions), `ClientChunkCacheMixin_LightPillars` (`ClientChunkCache.onLightUpdate`, light computed on the client), `ClientPacketListenerMixin_LightPillars` (`readSectionList`, light from the server), and Forge's `ChunkEvent`. Counters in `/calculatedSky pillars`, no change in behavior.
- `8b8cdb663`: the light scan is an index of every loaded chunk that changes only on those events. `LightPillarWorkQueue`, free of client types, queues a chunk when its sky light arrives and a section when its sky light or an emitter in it changes. Clusters are kept per section with a `64` bit mask of used cells. A section is walked only if its own sky light data or the bottom row of the section above has a value above `8` (`LightEngineAccessor_LightPillars`). The chunk radius moved into `LightPillarSelection.choose`. This replaced the passes and the surface heights.
- Working tree, not yet committed: shadows. `LightPillarHorizons` keeps, for each of the `48` slots, `64` azimuth bins of `16` samples spaced quadratically out to `16 (sqrt(r^2 + r) + sqrt(2))` blocks for a chunk radius `r`. Each sample is the highest angle above the light's top at which blocks stop its light, found by a 2D DDA over block columns that skips the light's own column and treats unloaded columns as open. Block and chunk changes mark only the bins they can reach, and dirty bins are rebuilt on the client tick, up to `16384` columns a tick. `LightPillarOccluders` decides what stops light, cached per block state. `LightPillarRenderer` passes each slot's horizon toward the eye in `lights[slot].w`, and `light_pillars.glsl` and `LightPillarOptics` drop the crystals whose light leaves the light below it. The screen-space rule from the previous entry and the cloud height uniform are gone.

## Decisions

- empireu decided on `2026-11-13` to index every loaded chunk, to hold a slot's fade-in until its horizons are built, and that glass does not stop light. In this step blocks without collision (plants, fluids, torches, read through `BlockBehaviourAccessor_LightPillars`) and the blocks in `passes_light.csv` (glass, panes, and clear ice) let light through, and every other block stops it at the top of its outline shape.
- Embeddium, which runs in the dev client and the pack, overwrites `LevelRenderer.setSectionDirty` and its siblings, so the hooks sit on the client level, the chunk cache, and the packet listener instead.
- Standing still in empireu's world, the counters of the first step showed about `1,670` block changes a second, mostly `frostedheart:dirt_permafrost` and `frostedheart:gravel_permafrost` from FrostedHeart's own server code, and lava and fire changing state at lights. So the scan skips changes that keep the block and its emission, and the horizons skip changes whose old and new state stop light at the same height.
- A light's top is the top of its outline shape, or of its fluid, else half a block. The horizon is measured from there, so a lantern or a campfire does not shadow itself.
- The cut is `highestTilt = min(highestTilt, (e - horizon) / 2)`: a crystal at elevation `e` with its face tilted by `tau` gets light that left the light at `e - 2 tau`. The lowest ribbon row starts at `horizon - 8 sigma`. The cut from the scene depth on the eye side stays.
- The GameTests of the last two steps were written by Subordinate, a headless subagent, from specs by Claude, and then reviewed and fixed by Claude.

## Validation

- After the first step, in `runClient`: the counters moved with changes and stayed at `0` otherwise, apart from the permafrost changes.
- After the second step: `33` of `33` GameTests. At the spot of the run before it, the same lights were chosen (`13` against `11`, the `2` extra not identified). Standing still: `0` sections tested, `0` bytes allocated, and `0.009 ms` per tick against `0.120 ms` for the passes. empireu checked placing, breaking, and covering a torch, a torch in a deep shaft, relogging, a teleport, and turning the sky off and on. A dimension change was not tested, since the pack has only one dimension.
- A read-only review of the shadows by Subordinate (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash with high reasoning) led to four fixes. The bin rebuilds start each tick at the slot where the last tick ran out of budget, so a stream of changes cannot starve the later slots. A slot freed by the selection keeps its last horizons, since it still fades out during that tick and was drawn without a shadow. A tag reload marks every bin, not only the cached heights. A block whose shape throws counts as stopping light at its top instead of stopping all pillars. A new test checks the wrap between bin 63 and bin 0 with three bins that differ. The review also noted that horizons are measured from the light's top while the shader places the light at its center, which is on purpose: light leaves through the top of a block, and a cluster takes the highest top of its lights.
- After the shadows, on `2026-11-13`: `runGameTestServer -PgameTestNamespaces=frostedheart_calculated_sky` with JDK 17 passes all `42` tests. The GameTest structures sit at `y -60` under the terrain, so the tests that read the level build their scenes at `y 300`. A new test of ribbon edges above a horizon found up to `1.0114` times the visible value outside the ribbon, so rows are now also as wide as the half rows next to them. The worst is `0.971` above a horizon and `0.980` without one. Both shader stages pass `glslangValidator`.
- In game on `2026-11-13`, empireu's test scene of seven lights, which all showed pillars before this step: the glowstone in the open and the torches in a 1-high and a 2-high glass box keep their pillars. The torches in a 1-high and a 2-high ring of logs, the torch behind a log, and the torch under a tree show none in a level view, and looking up shows a faint pillar higher up, as a 1-high ring cuts only below about `37` degrees. The torch under the tree is a false shadow: `highestStop` takes the highest block of a column, so the leaves above count as a wall from the ground, while the light toward the eye passes below them. A torch in glass under a log roof, seen from about `3` blocks, showed a pillar over the roof. It comes from crystals within a block of the eye, lit by light leaving the torch at `22` to `34` degrees below the roof, which the model gets right, but so few crystals would show as sparkles and not as a smooth band. Building and breaking walls, pistons, `/fill`, TNT, the counters when standing still, relogging, and Esc were not checked before this commit.
- Documentation: so far only the sentence on half rows. The sections on finding and drawing the lights still describe the passes and the screen-space rule.
- The full `frostedheart` GameTest namespace was not run for this entry.
